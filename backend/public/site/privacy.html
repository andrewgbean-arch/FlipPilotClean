Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026 · Version 1

This explains what FlipPilot does with your information. We have tried to write it so you can actually read it. Where it says "we", it means FlipPilot (see section 1). Where it says "the app", it means the FlipPilot mobile app and the pages on flippilot.co.uk.

The short version

  • To make an account we ask for your email address and nothing else. There is no password, and we don't send marketing emails.
  • A photo you scan is sent to an AI service (OpenAI) to work out what the item is. We don't keep it.
  • Your saved scans, photos and history stay on your phone, not on our servers.
  • What you choose to put on the Marketplace (listings, photos, your display name, reviews) is public. Messages are private between you and the other person.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't use it for advertising profiles, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • In the app, Settings lets you download everything we hold about you, or delete your account.

1. Who we are

FlipPilot is run by Andrew Bean, trading as FlipPilot, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of the personal information described here, which means we decide how and why it is used.

You can contact us at support@flippilot.co.uk about anything in this policy, including to ask about your information or to use any of your rights (section 8).

3. Why we are allowed to use your information

  • To give you what you asked for (a contract with you): your account, scans, Marketplace, messages, credits and Pro.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the Marketplace and the service safe and secure, stopping fraud and abuse, fixing faults, and counting adverts. We weigh this against your rights. You can object (section 8).
  • The law: when we are required to keep or hand over something, for example in answer to a lawful request.
  • Your consent: optional location for the weather card and for local adverts. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time.

We use some automatic checks. For example, a car listing is refused if its registration isn't recognised or the make doesn't match, and a listing that looks suspicious can be flagged. If one of these gets it wrong, email us and a person will look at it.

4. Who else handles your information

We use these companies to run FlipPilot, and we send each only what its job needs. They also have their own privacy policies.

  • Render (render.com) hosts our server. Our data is kept on a disk in Frankfurt, Germany. Render takes a daily backup of that disk and keeps each backup for at least seven days, so something you delete can remain in a backup for a short time afterwards. Requests to our server pass through Render's network, which uses Cloudflare.
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails. We use its Ireland servers. It keeps a log of the emails it sends, including the code in them, for a limited time under its own terms.
  • Cloudflare holds our website address, forwards emails sent to support@flippilot.co.uk to us, and hosts our main website at flippilot.co.uk, so it sees your IP address when your browser loads a page from there.
  • OpenAI looks at the photos you scan, and at item names, advert text and advert pictures, to identify items and to help check adverts. We use its business API.
  • eBay supplies public listings for price guides, and is the service you connect if you export an item to eBay.
  • SerpApi lets us search Google Shopping for some items. Open Food Facts supplies food and grocery barcode details.
  • Product pictures. When you scan a barcode, the picture shown with the result comes from eBay's or Open Food Facts' servers. Your phone loads it from them directly, so they see your phone's IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on the Operation Nightglass story pages. The request goes from your phone straight to Google, not through us.
  • The DVLA and DVSA are UK government services that supply vehicle and MOT details.
  • RevenueCat keeps track of subscriptions and purchases for us, with Google Play and the App Store, who take payment.
  • Open-Meteo gives the weather and postcodes.io turns postcodes into places. The weather request goes from your phone straight to Open-Meteo, not through us, and carries your phone's approximate location.
  • Stripe takes card payments from businesses that buy adverts. It is not used for ordinary app users.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people from harm, or to deal with a legal claim. If FlipPilot is ever sold, your information would go with it, and the same promises would carry on.

5. Sending information outside the UK

Some of these companies are based in the USA or use servers outside the UK. When your information goes to a provider outside the UK, we rely on a safeguard the law allows, such as the UK–US data bridge for companies certified under it, or standard contract terms with the UK addendum, and we send only what is needed. Our own data sits in Germany and Ireland.

6. Adverts

The app shows adverts from businesses, always labelled as sponsored. They are not based on a profile of you. At most the app uses the rounded location described above to show a nearby business, and counts how often an advert is shown. If you report an advert, we keep your report (the reason, your note and your ID) for up to {{reportMonths}} months, or until a person has looked at the advert again and cleared it, with your identity taken off if you delete your account. Businesses that advertise use a separate portal, and our advertiser terms explain what we hold about them: their email, business details and booking, with card details going to Stripe and never to us.

7. Car dealers and trade sellers

Some Marketplace sellers are businesses. A seller can tell us it sells as a business and is then labelled "Trade seller" with the business name it gives. Some dealerships list their cars through FlipPilot Dealer OS, our software for dealers. If you message one, it sees your message and a reference number for the chat. It does not see your email or device ID, and it is responsible for what it does with your enquiry. Dealer OS has its own privacy policy for dealers and their staff.

8. Your rights and choices

You have the right to see what we hold about you, to have mistakes corrected, to have your information deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to take a copy with you. You can do most of this yourself:

  • Download your information: Settings, then Your data, then Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. See what gets deleted.
  • Anything else: email support@flippilot.co.uk. We aim to answer within a month. We may need to check it is really you first.

If you aren't happy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Sign-in codes and sign-in tokens are stored only in scrambled form, eBay sign-in tokens are encrypted, and the device ID is never shown to other users. We only let in the people who need to see the data to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in codes to yourself, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

10. Children

FlipPilot is for people aged 18 or over, and the app asks you to confirm that when you start. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

11. Cookies and tracking

The pages on flippilot.co.uk set no cookies and have no analytics or advertising trackers, and they load nothing from other companies. The advertiser portal (api.flippilot.co.uk/advertise) keeps a sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in, and uses it for nothing else. The app doesn't use advertising IDs or tracking SDKs. Apart from the services in section 4 (the weather, postcode look-ups, product pictures, the story-page lettering and purchases through the app store), the only other places your phone goes are links you choose to tap, such as a map.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, where it affects what you have agreed to, ask you to confirm the new version in the app. Earlier versions are available on request.

Questions about any of this? Email support@flippilot.co.uk.
