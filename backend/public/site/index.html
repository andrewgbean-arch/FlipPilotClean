What FlipPilot does

Scan and price

Scan a barcode, or photograph anything without one, and FlipPilot works out what it is. You get a price range built from real eBay listings and shop prices, with a suggested buy price and sell price.

Track your flips

Save items to your History, mark what you actually bought and what you paid, and see your real profit.

Sell nearby

Browse the Marketplace for free and list an item with photos, a price and a description. Message buyers and sellers in the app, and check seller ratings and reviews.

Cars and MOT

Look up any UK vehicle by its registration for its MOT history and DVLA details. Keep your own vehicles' MOT dates in one place, and list a car for sale.

Boot fairs

Find boot fairs, markets and fêtes near you, see dates and times, check the weather before you set off, and add your own event.

Free to use

Sign up with your email address (there is no password) and get {{freeScans}} scans a month. An optional Pro plan adds unlimited scanning within fair use, extra shop-price checks on pricier items and export to eBay.

Good to know. Prices are estimates based on asking prices in public listings, not sold prices or a valuation, and photo recognition can be wrong, so always check before you buy. FlipPilot is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, eBay or any other marketplace or retailer. You must be 18 or over to use FlipPilot.
