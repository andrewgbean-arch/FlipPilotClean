The small print, in plain English

Booked by the month

Paid monthly in advance, no long contract. Stop any time and you keep your slot until the month you paid for runs out.

Every advert is checked

We review the wording and pictures before anything goes live. If we can’t approve it you aren’t charged, and we’ll tell you what to change.

Clearly marked

Every advert says “Sponsored”, and anyone can report one. No fake reviews, star ratings or guarantees.

Honest numbers

You get a report of how many times your advert was shown, tapped and saved for later, by day, as counted by the app. We are new and audiences grow, so we don’t promise a number of views. That is why launch prices are half price, and why, if your first month is quieter than we’d both like, we add a month free.

Your area

Local adverts are shown to people within your chosen distance of your postcode. The app uses a rounded position, from phones that have already shared it, only to choose adverts, and we don’t store it.

Fair rotation

Nobody is shown the same advertiser over and over. Each phone sees the advertiser it saw longest ago, and the cycle restarts once everyone booked in that area has had a turn.

All prices are per month. We are not currently VAT registered, so no VAT is added. Prices and the launch offer may change for new bookings; a booking you’ve made keeps its price for the months you’ve paid for. Emails you send us are used only to answer you.
