FlipPilot · Dealer OS

Run your dealership from one place.

FlipPilot Dealer OS is software for UK car dealers: your stock, your diary, your team and your customers together, with a phone app your staff can use on the forecourt. Ask for a demo and we’ll show you it working with a dealership like yours.

What it does today

Stock and photos

Every vehicle in one list with its details and photos, and a public page for each car with a QR code you can put on the windscreen.

Diary, rota and jobs

Who is in, what is booked, and a job board the whole team can see. Staff clock in and out from their phones.

Customers and wanted cars

A customer database, and a “tell me when you get one” form so people can ask for the car they want and you can see it.

Invoices and the books

Invoices and simple bookkeeping that understands VAT, with the profit on each car worked out honestly.

MOT and reminders

Keep track of MOT and service dates so nothing is missed.

Pilot Brain, your AI assistant

Ask questions about your own business in plain English. It is for owners and managers only, and it never sees wages or private messages.

We are building this with dealers, so it grows quickly. If something you need isn’t here yet, tell us: it shapes what we build next.
